# Obsidian Generator

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <ItemImage id="deepnullreforged:obsidian_generator_upgrade" />
</Column>

Generates obsidian in a DampNull. Unlike the Stone Generator, this one consumes both water and lava.

## Availability

- DampNull only
- Every tier

## Tier Differences

| Tier | Rate | Fluid Cost |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| All tiers | 1 per 5 seconds | 1,000 mB water + 1,000 mB lava |
