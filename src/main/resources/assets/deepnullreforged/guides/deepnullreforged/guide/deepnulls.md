![DeepNull GUI](images/deepnull-and-tiers.png)

DeepNulls are the item-storage half of DeepNull Reforged. Each slot locks to one item type and can hold far more than a normal stack, while the selected slot can be placed or used directly from the item in your hand.

## Core Capabilities

- Large per-slot item storage
- Per-slot extraction and placement rules
- Direct use of stored blocks, buckets, food, potions, and many other right-click items
- Automatic pickup of matching stored items
- Docking station automation support
- JEI crafting transfer support
