# Deep Energy Upgrade

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <ItemImage id="deepnullreforged:deep_energy_upgrade" />
</Column>

Deep Energy is the higher-end energy upgrade for Emerald DeepNulls. It shares the energy slot with the base Energy Upgrade and massively boosts FE capacity and transfer.

## Availability

- DeepNull only
- Emerald tier only

## Tier Differences

| Tier | Capacity | Transfer |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Emerald | 25,000,000 FE | 100,000 FE/t |
