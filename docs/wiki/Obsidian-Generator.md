# Obsidian Generator

The Obsidian Generator produces obsidian inside a DampNull and actively consumes stored water and lava.

## Availability

- DampNull only
- Every tier

## Notes

- Generates `1 obsidian / 5s`
- Consumes `1,000 mB` of water and `1,000 mB` of lava per output
- Shares the same upgrade family slot as Stone Generator

See JEI or GuideME for the current recipe.
