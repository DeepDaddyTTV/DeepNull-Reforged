# Auto-Smelting Upgrade

Auto-Smelting processes supported world pickups before they enter a DeepNull.

## Availability

- DeepNull only
- Every tier

## Notes

- Applies to world pickup, not docking or transfer actions
- Excludes ore blocks
- Works with Stoneworks to produce glass from sand

See JEI or GuideME for the current recipe.
