# Deep Energy Upgrade

Deep Energy is the stronger FE storage option for DeepNulls.

## Availability

- DeepNull only
- Emerald tier

## Tier Differences

| Tier | Capacity | Transfer |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Emerald | 25,000,000 FE | 100,000 FE/t |

## Notes

- Shares the same upgrade family slot as Energy Upgrade
