# Filter Upgrade

The Filter Upgrade adds ghost-item filtering to DeepNulls. It controls what can enter matching slots and gives access to whitelist, blacklist, and preset filter modes.

## Availability

- DeepNull only
- Iron tier and above

## Notes

- Uses the separate [Filter Item](Filter-Item) in its recipe
- Lets matching items be accepted more predictably during automation and pickup
